Pottery class FAQs

Clear answers for students visiting Dodream Ceramic Studio in New Westminster, serving Vancouver and Metro Vancouver.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Dodream Ceramic Studio located?

#33 – 800 McBride Blvd., New Westminster, BC V3L 2B8. We welcome students from Vancouver and across Metro Vancouver.

Do I need pottery experience?

No experience is needed for the one-day pottery class. Clay, glaze, firing, and guidance are included.

How long does a one-day pottery class take?

The one-day pottery experience is 90 minutes. Weekend sessions are at 11:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 4:00 PM. Weekday sessions are available by flexible booking upon request.

When will finished pottery pieces be ready?

Completed pieces take about four weeks. We will notify you by SMS when they are ready, and pieces are kept for three months from the SMS notification date.

How do I book a pottery class?

Use our booking form to share your preferred class, date, time, and group size. We will reply to confirm availability.
